Ballast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Ballast is a personal journalling app. It is designed to keep your writing on your own device. There is no Ballast account, no Ballast server, and no Ballast backend — there is nowhere for your entries to be sent, because no such service exists.

What stays on your device

Your journal entries and your activity list are stored as ordinary files in a BallastData folder on your device. They are never uploaded, synced, backed up, or transmitted anywhere by the app. Nothing in the app reads your entries and sends them elsewhere.

If you choose to sync that folder yourself — with Syncthing, a cloud drive, or a manual copy — that transfer is governed by whichever tool you used, not by Ballast.

What can leave your device

One feature transmits data, and only when you explicitly ask it to: the in-app feedback form. If you fill it in and tap submit, Ballast creates an issue in the developer's issue tracker on GitHub containing:

DataWhy
The title and description you typedSo the bug or request can be understood and acted on
The app versionTo know which build the report concerns
Your browser/OS user-agent stringTo reproduce platform-specific problems

Nothing else is attached — no journal entries, no activity data, no logs, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no location. The request goes to api.github.com over HTTPS and to no other host. The app's network permissions are restricted at the operating-system level to that single address, so it is not technically capable of contacting anywhere else.

This feature also requires you to supply your own GitHub access token before it will do anything, so it is inert unless you have deliberately configured it. That token is stored in the app's private configuration area on your device, is never written into the BallastData folder, and is sent only to GitHub as an authorisation header. You can remove it at any time from the "More" screen.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No crash or error reporting to any service
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No accounts, sign-in, or profiles
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data held to sell

Children

Ballast is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone, whatever their age.

Deleting your data

Your entries are yours and local: delete the BallastData folder, or individual files inside it, and they are gone. Note that on some platforms this folder lives outside the app's own storage, so uninstalling Ballast may leave it in place — delete it directly if you want it removed.

If you submitted feedback and want it deleted, email the address below and the corresponding issue will be removed.
